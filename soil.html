Can We Collect What's Transparent?

Art installation and performance (Uppsala, 2026)

Can We Collect What's Transparent? is a site-specific art installation and performance where a metal sculpture is placed in nature. The sculpture is then dressed in a long transparent fabric carried by the dancers, creating a space for the dancers to walk through and stand under, collectively transform into a part of the sculpture. They become a voice relating to each other, the materials, and the nature around them.
It asks questions of where our bodies end and the surroundings begin. What can be seen through the material? Can the outside be altered or experienced in a different way by seeing it from a different view? Through a new surface?
The meeting point of the soft fabric and the hard metal offers a resonating sensation of floating while also being grounded. The transparent fabric shows the movement of the wind and gives the dancers a possibility follow the movement seen in the floating, moving textile. Giving agency to relate to the nature around them, while also listening to each others bodies to be in the space together.

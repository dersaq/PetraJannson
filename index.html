About me

GET TO KNOW ME

A life shaped
by movement.

My name is Petra Janssen. I am a professional dancer, born in Sweden and now based in Berlin.

Throughout my journey, I have had the opportunity to work and grow as an artist in Berlin, a city whose creative energy continues to inspire me.

Dance is my way of expressing my passion for art. Through movement, I explore new ideas, challenge my own limits and constantly reinvent myself — giving shape to the things that words cannot express.

Movement is my language.

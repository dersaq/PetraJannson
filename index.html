About Petra Jansson

Petra Jansson is a movement and visual artist from Sweden whose practice unfolds at the intersection of performance, dance, sculpture, painting and installation. Her work explores natural materials through looking at the body as an entity of nature in conversation with the world around us. Through sculpture and installation their work builds community and practices where bodies meet as a form of connection to ourselves, others, the sounds around, and the textures we feel.

Delving into questions of queerness, identity and community, Petra connects the practice of deep listening and improvisation in her artwork. Aiming to convey nature as a part of us, seeing a close connection between humans and the earth, they encourage people to love and protect the nature we are in.

Petra received their BA in Creative Arts Contemporary Dance at Trinity Laban Conservatoire of Music and Dance (2021). Additionally they have completed courses such as Sustainable Development (2018), Gender Studies (2021, 2022), Indigenous Feminism (2022), Feminist Postcolonial and Critical Race Studies (2023), and Queer Studies (2023).
